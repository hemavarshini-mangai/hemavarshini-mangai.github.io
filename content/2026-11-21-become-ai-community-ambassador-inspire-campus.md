**Introduction**

Learning Artificial Intelligence becomes more meaningful when students learn, experiment, and share knowledge together. While individual learning is valuable, being part of a community creates opportunities to exchange ideas, explore new technologies, work on projects, and grow together.

To encourage collaborative AI learning, we are building a network of college-based AI learning communities through our chapter program. We are inviting enthusiastic students to become **Chapter Ambassadors** and take the lead in bringing AI learners together within their colleges.

This is an opportunity to connect with like-minded students, develop leadership skills, participate in open-source activities, and help create an active AI learning environment on campus.

---

**1. What Is an AI Learning Chapter?**

An AI learning chapter is a local student community where individuals come together to explore Artificial Intelligence, exchange knowledge, participate in activities, and work on practical initiatives.

Each chapter is managed by a host or coordinator, supported by ambassadors from different colleges who encourage student participation and help organize regular meetups.

The goal is to create a welcoming environment where beginners and experienced learners can interact, share their knowledge, and discover new opportunities in AI.

**Our Initial Chapter Locations**

We are beginning with three locations:

- **Chennai**
- **Coimbatore**
- **Trichy** – Coming soon

Each chapter will organize a meetup every two weeks. Sessions can take place offline or online, making it possible for students to participate regardless of their location.

---

**2. Who Is a Chapter Ambassador?**

A Chapter Ambassador represents the AI learning community within their college.

Ambassadors play an important role in connecting students with the chapter, welcoming newcomers, encouraging participation, and supporting the host or coordinator during meetups.

Every chapter requires at least one active ambassador each month to maintain engagement and ensure that students feel included.

**Responsibilities of an Ambassador**

As an ambassador, you will:

- Introduce the chapter and invite students from your college to participate.
- Welcome new members and help them feel comfortable during meetups.
- Coordinate with the chapter host or coordinator to organize sessions.
- Assist with basic meetup arrangements, including snacks.
- Encourage students to participate in AI discussions, fun activities, and games.
- Motivate members to explore and contribute to open-source projects.
- Help create an interactive and friendly learning environment.

**Ambassador Stipend**

Ambassadors will receive a stipend ranging from **INR 500 to INR 2,000**.

The opportunity also provides practical experience in communication, coordination, teamwork, and community leadership.

---

**3. How Does a Chapter Meetup Work?**

Each meetup is designed to be short, engaging, and interactive.

A session will last a maximum of **50 minutes**, with a focus on building connections and learning together.

**Meetup Activities**

1. **Random Fun Challenge:** An interactive activity to encourage participation and help members connect with one another.
2. **AI Talk:** A short session covering an AI-related topic, technology, or interesting development.
3. **Fun Games:** Group activities designed to make the meetup enjoyable and engaging.
4. **Open-Source Discussions:** Conversations about contributing to open-source projects, sharing ideas, and exploring collaboration opportunities.

Meetups can be conducted online or offline depending on the chapter's arrangements.

**Attendance Tracking**

Attendance will be recorded for every session to track member participation and overall chapter activity. This will also support the evaluation of participation hours and recognition milestones.

---

**4. Certificates, Stipends, and Recognition**

The chapter program encourages consistent participation by offering certificates, ambassador benefits, and performance-based recognition.

**Participation Certificate**

Members who complete a minimum of **50 hours** of chapter activity become eligible for a participation certificate.

At least **5 hours must be dedicated to open-source contributions**.

**Ambassador Bonus**

Ambassadors who complete 50 hours of work will receive an additional bonus on top of their regular stipend.

**100-Hour Milestone Recognition**

For every 100 hours of completed work, one member will be selected for milestone recognition.

**Quarterly Top Performer**

At the end of every quarter, one outstanding performer will be selected from across all chapters.

The selected member will receive:

- A certificate of recognition.
- A bonus.
- A prize worth **INR 5,000**.

**First Evaluation**

The first performance evaluation will take place at the end of the initial three-month period.

This evaluation will help recognize active members and ambassadors based on their participation and contributions.

**5. Why Should You Become an Ambassador?**

Becoming an ambassador is more than just representing a student community. It is an opportunity to develop skills, build meaningful connections, and contribute to a growing AI ecosystem.

**Build Your Network**

Connect with students from different colleges who share an interest in Artificial Intelligence, programming, and emerging technologies.

**Improve Communication Skills**

Organizing meetups, welcoming participants, and coordinating activities will help you become more confident in communicating with groups.

**Develop Leadership Experience**

Take responsibility for bringing students together, coordinating activities, and supporting community initiatives.

**Gain Open-Source Experience**

Explore collaborative development, learn from other contributors, and build a record of open-source participation.

**Earn Recognition and Rewards**

Become eligible for participation certificates, ambassador stipends, additional bonuses, milestone recognition, and the quarterly top performer prize.

**Create an AI Community in Your College**

Help students discover AI opportunities and create a space where they can learn, collaborate, and grow together.

---

**6. Who Can Apply?**

Students who enjoy learning, sharing knowledge, and connecting with others are encouraged to apply.

You do not need to be an AI expert to become an ambassador. What matters is your interest in technology, willingness to take responsibility, and enthusiasm for bringing people together.

A good ambassador should be:

- Interested in Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies.
- Comfortable interacting with fellow students.
- Willing to coordinate and support community activities.
- Enthusiastic about encouraging others to participate.
- Responsible and consistent in their involvement.

The most important quality is the willingness to build an active and supportive learning community.

---

**7. How to Apply**

If you are interested in becoming a Chapter Ambassador, we would love to hear from you.

When applying, share the following details:

- Your name.
- Your college name.
- The chapter you would like to be associated with.
- Why you are interested in becoming an ambassador.
- How you plan to encourage students from your college to participate.

Your application should highlight your interest in community building, communication, leadership, and AI learning.
